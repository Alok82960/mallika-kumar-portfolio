About

A Lifelong Commitment to Education & Cooperation

Academic leader, policy advisor, and global advocate bridging scholarship with sustainable impact.

Dr. Mallika Kumar is Professor of Economics and Founder Coordinator of the Office of International Programmes (OIP) at Shri Ram College of Commerce (SRCC), University of Delhi. With 33 years of teaching experience, her research centers on Cooperatives, Women Empowerment and the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

She completed the Harvard University Professional Development Course on 'Managing Yourself and Leading Others'. Her publications appear in refereed and Scopus-indexed journals. She has been nominated by the National Cooperative Union of India (NCUI) as an expert for research scholarships in Cooperatives.

Dr. Kumar was invited to speak on Cooperation and Education at the soft launch of the UN International Year of Cooperatives on July 9, 2024 at the United Nations Headquarters, New York, and presented at the VNR Lab on SDG 5: Gender Equality at the High-Level Political Forum, UN HQ, July 15, 2025.

Beyond academia, she advances her mission of connecting the local to the global by linking students, researchers and women entrepreneurs with NITI Aayog's Women Entrepreneur Platform, the Ministry of Cooperation, the United Nations and the World Bank. She advises Saksham, a society supporting the visually impaired, and plays the Sitar in her free time.
